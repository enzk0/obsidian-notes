## Privacy
---
Deepfakes undermines control over identity and image, and can infringe the privacy rights of a person. As the person's likeness, face, or voice can be misused by other people without consent (Pawan)(Chidera). Moreover, deepfakes can also misuse the personal data that other people shared online for purposes that they never intended, making any ordinary online data as a means for manipulation. While in more serious cases, deepfakes are used for nonconsensual pornographic material or image-based abuse, where the victim's media, may it be photo or video, is manipulated for malicious content that can be traced back to them. The privacy concerns regarding deepfakes is harmful as it includes blackmailing, extortion, and reputational damage
