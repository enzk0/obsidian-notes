

## Gender and Education
***Education***
- a basic human right that is essential for the progress of humanity
- The Right to Universal Primary Education hopes to eliminate *gender disparity* in primary and secondary education.
- gender parity does not mean gender equality

## The Importance of Education: An International Perspective
***Article 26*** of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- stresses that education is everyone's fundamental right. A necessary thing to fulfill one's human potential.
- specifically primary education is a non-negotiable right that must be free for all while technical and professional education, a long with the higher education should be accessible and based on one's merit and skills.
- The 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child echoes this right

***The Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW)***
- necessitates the elimination of discrimination against women.
- + the elimination on discriminatory acts against women in institutions of education.

### Education and the Beijing Platform for Action (BPfA) in 1995
Education is a human right and an essential tool for achieving the goals of gender equality, development, and peace. It is a *strategic tool* for gender equality that was solidified in the 1995 BPfA.
